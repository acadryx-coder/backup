⚡ Everything you need

More than a result app — it's a full school OS.

Acadryx started as a result engine. Today, it's a complete SIS + SMS + LMS with features you can toggle on/off in one click.

📊

Results & Grading

Teachers enter scores from any device. Weighted totals, custom grading scales, PDF report cards with 36 state templates — automatically published to parents.

💰

Bursary & Finance

Fee templates, class‑arm billing, per‑student add‑ons, payment receipts, expense tracking, and defaulter notifications. All in one place.

💻

CBT Exams

Question banks, auto‑graded exams, random question selection, time limits, shuffle options, and one‑click score mapping to term assessments.

📅

Attendance

Daily and subject‑based attendance with real‑time auto‑save, parent notifications, and detailed reports. Know who's in class at a glance.

🎓

Alumni Network

Every graduate gets a lifelong alumni account. They can network, find classmates, and stay connected — turning your school into a lifelong community.

⚙️

Feature toggles

Don't need library management? Turn it off. Want hostel attendance? Turn it on. Acadryx adapts to your school, not the other way around.
